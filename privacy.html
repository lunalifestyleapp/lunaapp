Privacy Policy

Luna — Cycle Planner

Effective 20 July 2026

Your data never leaves your phone.

no accounts · no analytics · no tracking · no ads

§ What Luna collects

Nothing. Luna has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking technologies and no third-party software development kits. We do not collect, receive, store, share or sell any personal information — because none of it ever reaches us.

§ What stays on your device

Everything you enter into Luna is stored only in the app's local storage on your own phone:

  • your cycle dates and any delay days you record,
  • your preferences — goal, activities, food allergies, menopause mode and similar settings,
  • your daily checklists, end-of-cycle answers, notes and history.

This information is never transmitted anywhere. We have no servers holding a copy and no technical ability to see it.

§ Network connections

The app makes no network calls. Fonts, artwork and all content are bundled inside the app itself, so Luna works fully offline and sends nothing over the internet.

§ Health information

Cycle dates and related wellbeing entries are sensitive by nature. They are treated exactly like everything else above: kept on your device, under your control, invisible to us and to anyone else. Luna does not connect to Apple Health or any other health platform.

§ Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your phone permanently deletes everything Luna stored. There are no cloud copies, so nothing survives removal and there is nothing to request from us.

§ The App Store and TestFlight

Luna is distributed through Apple's App Store and, during beta testing, through TestFlight. Apple may show us aggregated, anonymised statistics (such as download counts) and, only if you have opted in on your device, crash reports. That information is collected by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; it contains nothing you entered into Luna.

§ Children

Luna is not directed at children under 13, and since the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

§ Not medical advice

Luna offers general wellness guidance. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment — for medical concerns, please consult a healthcare professional.

§ Changes to this policy

If Luna's privacy practices ever change, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised before any change applies. Since the app currently collects nothing, any future change could only add clarity or introduce features that would be described here first.

§ Contact

Questions about privacy are welcome at luna.lifestyle.app@gmail.com.
